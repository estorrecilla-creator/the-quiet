Privacy Policy — ITW Publisher

Last updated: 2026-07-29

"ITW Publisher" is a private automation tool operated by a single owner to publish the owner's own original music video content to the owner's own social media accounts (including TikTok). It does not have public users, does not offer any service to third parties, and does not collect, process, or share personal information belonging to anyone other than its own owner/operator.

What this tool processes

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok when the owner authorizes this tool to publish on behalf of their own TikTok account. These tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are never transmitted to, or shared with, any third party.
  • Video files and captions created by the owner (music videos and short clips of the owner's own songs), uploaded directly to TikTok through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

What this tool does not do

  • It does not collect data from TikTok viewers, followers, or any other third party.
  • It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share any data with anyone.
